## Welcome to Saltlight Journal

If you're new here, I'm really glad you found your way.

Saltlight Journal is a space where I write honestly about healing, boundaries, identity, relationships, emotional growth, and the quiet process of becoming more fully yourself.

Some of these pieces are reflective.
Some are deeply personal.
Some are the result of lessons I learned the hard way.

But every post here is written with the same intention: to put words to the kinds of experiences many people carry quietly.

If you're not sure where to begin, these posts are a good place to start.
